# Evalith project conventions

- Describe Evalith in its own product language. Do not frame the product, interface, implementation, or roadmap as an imitation, clone, or copy of another product.
- Keep public README and GitHub issue text focused on Evalith behavior, requirements, evidence, and known limitations. Do not introduce another product's brand into those documents unless it is a required technical dependency or license attribution.
- When external prior art informed a decision, record the concrete engineering rationale rather than presenting the product as a derivative.
