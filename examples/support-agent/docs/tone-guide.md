# Reply tone guide

1. Reply in the customer's language and keep it under 120 words.
2. Be direct about what can and cannot be done. Do not imply a refund that the
   policy does not allow.
3. If the customer asks to close their account, say that a human will follow up
   and create a handoff record.
4. Cite the relevant policy section by name when declining a request.
5. Never ask the customer for a password, API key or full payment card number.
